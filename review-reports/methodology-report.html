Methodology Report

Project: QCEW Employment & Establishment Dynamics · Date: 2026-06-05 · Reviewer rules: .claude/rules/methodology-rules.md

Inferred design: Descriptive / exploratory. The code computes and visualizes over-the-year (OTY) levels and percentage changes from the QCEW; it implements no causal identification strategy (no DiD, RD, IV, matching, or panel FE). The standard-error, clustering, and multiple-testing rules do not apply because no inferential estimates are produced. Findings below therefore concentrate on sample definition, variable usage, and descriptive-analysis disclosure, per the "err on the side of flagging" directive.

Findings

MEDIUM MR-001 — Undocumented sample restriction: state != "53" (legacy)

The legacy establishment ranking chart silently excluded Washington (state != "53") with no comment. This is the classic replication-failure pattern. It was specific to the 2022 Q3 narrative and is not carried into the generalized pipeline; 05_figures_rankings.R documents the removal. Confirm this matches intent for future releases.

MEDIUM MR-002 — Population vintage vs. QCEW target quarter

tidycensus::get_estimates() returns Population Estimates Program data at a fixed vintage (default 2024, verified against tidycensus docs). The ranking charts threshold population >= 100,000 using that vintage while the employment/wage measures are 2025 Q4. The mismatch is small and defensible but should be disclosed in any writeup. Recommendation: set the vintage argument explicitly so the population year is recorded, not implicit.

LOW MR-003 — Discretionary display/sample parameters

Two discretionary choices affect what readers see: the ±25% color cap on the maps and the 100,000-population county filter. Both are legitimate but were undocumented in the legacy script. They are now centralized and named in 00_setup.R (pct_cap_num, pop_threshold_int) and the cap is stated in each map caption. Consider a one-line rationale in the writeup.

LOW MR-004 — Private-sector restriction (own_code == 5)

All pulls filter to private-sector ownership. Appropriate, but it means government employment/establishments are excluded from every figure. Now disclosed in PROJECT.md and README.md; keep that disclosure in public-facing outputs.

LOW MR-005 — No external validation of pulled totals

The pipeline does not cross-check pulled national aggregates against the published BLS QCEW news release for the target quarter. For a near-census this is low risk, but a one-line spot-check (national private employment/establishment counts vs. the BLS summary) would catch a malformed pull early. Recommended as a lightweight QA step in 01_build_dataset.R.

INFO MR-006 — Survey weights not applicable

The QCEW is a near-universal administrative count derived from unemployment-insurance records, not a sample survey. The survey-weight rules (MR-VU1/VU2) do not apply: there are no sampling weights to use or misuse. Recorded explicitly so the absence of weights is not later mistaken for an omission.

INFO MR-007 — County map subsetting relies on the geometry join

The county map dataset applies no explicit aggregation-level filter; it relies on the join to county geometry (5-digit GEOID) to retain only county rows. This works because state/national/MSA area codes do not collide with 5-digit county FIPS, but an explicit agglvl_code filter on the county total would make the intent self-evident and guard against future QCEW layout changes.

Note: the document-consistency and number-verification reviews are not applicable — no manuscript or numbered writeup is present in the repository (the legacy tweet drafts are archived under archive/ and contain 2022 Q3 figures only).
